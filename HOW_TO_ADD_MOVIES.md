# 🎬 How to Add Movies to Vision Board

## Quick Guide

### Step 1: Browse Movies
1. Open the Streamify app
2. Browse movies on the **Home** tab or use **Search**
3. Tap on any movie to see its details

### Step 2: Add to Vision Board
1. On the movie details page, you'll see two buttons:
   - **"Add to Watchlist"** - Simple bookmark
   - **"Add to Vision Board"** - Planned watch order 🎯

2. Tap **"Add to Vision Board"** button (red gradient)

### Step 3: Set Priority & Notes
A beautiful modal will slide up with:

**Priority Options:**
- 🔥 **Must Watch** (High) - Red gradient
  - For movies you're dying to watch
  - Will appear at the top of your board
  
- ⭐ **Soon** (Medium) - Yellow gradient  
  - Movies you'll watch in the near future
  - Default selection
  
- ⏰ **Someday** (Low) - Green gradient
  - Movies for when you have time
  - Nice to have on your list

**Notes (Optional):**
- Add personal notes like:
  - "Friend recommended this"
  - "Perfect for weekend"
  - "Been on my list for years"

### Step 4: Confirm
Tap **"Add to Vision Board"** button at the bottom

✅ Success! Movie is now in your Vision Board

## 📊 Vision Board vs Watchlist Comparison

| Feature | Watchlist 📚 | Vision Board 🎯 |
|---------|-------------|----------------|
| **Purpose** | Simple collection | Planned marathon |
| **Organization** | Unordered list | Numbered sequence (#1, #2, #3...) |
| **Priority Levels** | ❌ No | ✅ Yes (High/Medium/Low) |
| **Personal Notes** | ❌ No | ✅ Yes |
| **Watch Order** | ❌ Random | ✅ Intentional |
| **Visual Design** | Basic list | Beautiful grid/list views |
| **Reordering** | ❌ No | ✅ Yes |
| **Use Case** | "Movies I might watch" | "My movie marathon plan" |

## 💡 When to Use Each

### Use **Watchlist** when:
- ✅ You want to quickly bookmark movies
- ✅ You're not sure about watch order
- ✅ You just want a simple collection
- ✅ You're browsing and saving for later

### Use **Vision Board** when:
- ✅ You're planning a movie marathon
- ✅ You want to prioritize what to watch first
- ✅ You have specific reasons for watching
- ✅ You want a beautiful visual plan
- ✅ You're organizing by mood/genre/priority

---

## 🚀 Pro Tips

### 1. **Start with Priorities**
   - Add your "Must Watch" movies first
   - These will guide your viewing journey

### 2. **Use Notes Effectively**
   - "Recommended by [friend name]"
   - "Perfect for [mood/occasion]"
   - "Part of [director] marathon"
   - "Sequel to [movie]"

### 3. **Mix Priorities**
   - Don't make everything "Must Watch"
   - Balance heavy dramas with light comedies
   - Mix genres for variety

### 4. **Regular Updates**
   - Remove movies after watching
   - Adjust priorities as your mood changes
   - Reorder based on availability

### 5. **View Modes**
   - Use **Grid View** for visual planning
   - Use **List View** for detailed information
   - Toggle between them with the buttons

## ❓ FAQ

**Q: Can I add the same movie to both Watchlist and Vision Board?**
A: Yes! They serve different purposes. Watchlist is for bookmarking, Vision Board is for planning.

**Q: Can I change the priority after adding?**
A: Yes! Tap on the movie in Vision Board and update its priority.

**Q: What happens to the order when I remove a movie?**
A: The remaining movies automatically reorder to fill the gap.

**Q: Can I have multiple Vision Boards?**
A: Currently one per user, but you can organize with priorities and notes.

**Q: Is there a limit to how many movies I can add?**
A: No limit! Add as many as you want.
